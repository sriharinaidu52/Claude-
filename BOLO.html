The Problem

Why do simple tasks still require so many steps?

Digital services are powerful, but using them often means opening the right app, finding the right feature, and manually entering the same details every time.

“I want to send ₹500 to Rahul.”
Open payment app→ Payments→ Send Money→ Select Rahul→ Enter ₹500→ Review→ Confirm
Users have to learn the interface before they can use the service.

The Solution

Just say what you want.

BOLO is designed as an intent-first interface — it listens, understands what you're asking for, and confirms before anything happens.

01

Voice input

Say what you need, naturally.

02

Language understanding

BOLO interprets the words and identifies the intent behind them.

03

Entity extraction

Details like recipient, amount or date are pulled out of the request.

04

Clarification

If anything is missing or unclear, BOLO asks — instead of guessing.

05

Confirmation

The action is shown clearly before it happens.

06

Secure authorization

Sensitive actions require appropriate secure authentication.

07

Action

The request is completed, or the user is guided through the flow.

Language

Speak the way you normally speak.

People shouldn't have to change the way they speak to use technology. These all represent the same underlying intent.

Telugu

“Rahul ki 500 pampu.”

English

“Send ₹500 to Rahul.”

Code-mixed

Code-mixed

BOLO is designed to support natural multilingual and code-mixed conversation — recognition improves over time and is never assumed to be perfect.

Built for the Real World

Voice doesn't work perfectly everywhere — so BOLO is designed around that.

Every real-world challenge below follows the same principle: when recognition is uncertain, BOLO clarifies instead of guessing. Tap a card for details.

Design Principle
BOLO is designed not around perfect voice recognition, but around safe and useful interaction when recognition is uncertain.
Trust & Security

Voice is the interface. Trust is the foundation.

Sensitive financial actions always require appropriate confirmation and secure authorization — voice alone is never treated as sufficient.

Your voice should not be your financial password.

Voice can potentially be imitated or spoofed. BOLO uses voice primarily to understand intent — identity and authorization rely on appropriate secure authentication mechanisms, not voice biometrics.
